## Matt in his own words

### On the firm & talent ("X-Men Academy")
- *"Sometimes I feel like I'm running the X-Men Academy."* On the brilliant-but-difficult: *"They create an absurd amount of chaos sometimes and you want to pull your hair out. But then you see what they can do and it's like, holy crap. Nobody else in the world could do that."*
- Bar: (via a colleague, but Matt's standard) *"Every single person must be in the top 1% of their domain."*

### Reading founders / investing
- On backing Zhang Yiming (ByteDance) through a translator, on nonverbal cues: *"I've got to figure out some way to back this person."*
- On ByteDance being his best-ever investment: *"increasingly zen about it… it definitely messes with your head that it's probably the best investment I'll ever make."*
- Sequoia as *"the highest-standards place I've ever experienced."* What it taught him: *"You start to see how far the axes extend, what a great founder looks like… Without being exposed to that, your whole perception of what's possible is missing the dynamic range of the top end."*
- On leaving Sequoia to start Paradigm: *"The realization that I can do it my own way was very liberating."*

### Crypto philosophy
- *"I internalized it as just a really beautiful idea."* On the cycles: *"You almost need to lose money the first time. Then you kind of give up on it, write it off as dead. And when you see it come back and it's not dead, then you start to wonder."*
- *"Money is upstream of a lot of the rest of crypto… Buying your first Bitcoin or setting up your first wallet is often the first step… It's like getting your AOL account and connecting to the internet for the first time."*
- *"Stablecoins are room-temperature superconductors for financial services."*
- On network adoption: *"It's like adopting a new language or settling a new city. It's useless if you do it alone."*
- Skepticism of authority (a through-line): *"I suppose I've always had somewhat of a skepticism towards authority, and so when I see authority exerting itself, it does make me wonder: is this the way we want the world to work?"* And: *"Everyone in the US looks at China and says, that looks dystopian. I don't think they fully realize the same thing is happening in the West."*

### Research-driven investing
- *"Most investment research is about gathering and analyzing existing information. We're trying to invent new things."*
- *"The biggest part of our process is figuring out what's actually the most important problem."*
- On open-sourcing even when it helps rivals: *"If someone else implemented this and it made things better for crypto overall, we'd be fine with that."*
- On lending against NFTs: *"If you can solve lending against NFTs, you can potentially solve lending against any illiquid asset."*

### The 2021 rebrand — his own retrospective (high value for the mission question)
- On dropping "crypto" from the homepage: *"We thought, okay, all the crypto people already know us, they never go to our homepage… So what's the big deal?"* → *"In hindsight, it was clearly a mistake. People take the website as a collective statement about what you're proud of."*
- The deeper lesson, in his words: *"When you over-focus on a rival, you become more like the rival."* And on the era's quality slip: *"We definitely let the quality bar slip… I remember making that compromise, feeling like if we don't do this, or hire that person, we're going to fall behind. In hindsight, those were all wrong decisions."*
- On down markets: *"Down years are easier than up years. Signal to noise is very high… with a long-term perspective, that doesn't bother us."*

### N=2 (worldview)
- *"Once you have an N equals two, that changes how you relate to everything."* (Tokyo/New York as a kid.)
