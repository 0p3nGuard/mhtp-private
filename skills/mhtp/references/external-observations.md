# External Observations of Matt — Held Lightly

**Read this framing first.** This file holds what *other people* observe about Matt — Dan Robinson, Alpin, Alana. It is deliberately kept separate from `huang-fidelity.md` (which is Matt's own words). This separation is a hard-won principle from the JMTP build: **do not encode other people's characterizations of a person as if they were that person's own lenses.**

**How MHTP uses this file:** as a source of *hypotheses to test*, never as fact about how Matt thinks. Frame anything drawn from here as a possibility — *"one thing others who know you have noticed… does that fit?"* — and let Matt confirm, refine, or reject. When an outside observation converges with something in Matt's own words, that convergence is worth noting; where it diverges, hold the observer's view the more lightly.
